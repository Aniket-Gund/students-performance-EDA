## 🌟 Key Insights Uncovered

1. **Test Preparation Advantage**: Students who completed test preparation courses achieved on average **10-15% higher scores** across all subjects.
2. **Parental Education Correlation**: A strong positive correlation between higher parental degrees and overall student academic performance.
3. **Subject Interdependence**: Reading and writing scores showed a high Pearson correlation ($r > 0.95$), indicating strong linguistic interdependence.
